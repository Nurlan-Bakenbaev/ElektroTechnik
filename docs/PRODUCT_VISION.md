# VOLT. — Interactive Elektrotechnik Academy

The product teaches German-speaking beginners and apprentices to interpret a circuit, predict an effect, measure it, explain it, calculate a missing quantity and recognize the limits of a model. Learning progress is evidence of local practice, not an official qualification.

The reference experience lives at `#/lesson/ohm`. Its primary surface is a working circuit and instruments. A step rail gives orientation; a light guidance panel presents one learning goal and the next action. The textbook becomes an optional reference tab rather than the default activity.

The platform keeps the current course IDs, profiles, bookmarks, five-question topic tests and 35-question final exam. Existing results remain compatible. Only the Ohm lesson currently meets the rebuilt lesson standard; the twelve remaining modules are an explicit migration backlog.

Success for a learner means predicting a current change, collecting consistent measurements, choosing a calculation method, using units correctly, and explaining why a wrong answer is implausible. Time spent reading and click count are not evidence of understanding.
